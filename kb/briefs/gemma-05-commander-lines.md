# Brief: commander voice lines

When one of the 8 leaders (see `kb/roster.md`) is the enemy **sector commander**, the game will show:
- **Taunt:** one line at the start of the battle, spoken to the player's squad.
- **Defeat:** one line when the commander's squad falls.

For each leader write **3 taunts and 2 defeat lines**. The game picks one at random.

Rules:
- At most 70 characters per line. The line is shown in a banner during play, so it has to be short.
- The voice comes from the leader's motto and blurb in `roster.md` (e.g. Banner Marshal "Hold the Line": proud, immovable). Echo the motto's attitude without repeating the motto word for word.
- Lean on faction voice:
  - Terran: military, terse, about ground and orders.
  - Ascendancy: clinical, polite, condescending, about data and protocol.
  - Manyworlds: tidal, communal, patient, organic metaphors.
  - Long Tomorrow: cosmic, fatalistic, burning, about time running out.
- No profanity, nothing that needs context the player doesn't have.

Format: a heading per leader, then "Taunts:" and "Defeat:" lists. Use the proposal header from `kb/README.md`.
