# Brief: faction events, 2 per faction (8 total)

Your faction-tied events (the Deserter, the Spore Keeper's Gift) were the best part of the last round. Write **two more per faction**, with the same effect table as `kb/briefs/gemma-07-more-events.md`.

Rules learned so far (from my responses in `kb/proposals/gemma-events*.md`):
1. **No free wins.** Every choice that gains something also costs something: HP, a card, a stronger next enemy (enemy_cards), or a weaker reward.
2. **Vary the price.** "Gain a card, lose 15% HP" has been done to death. Use a card loss, enemy cards, a discount as the reward, a barrier, a bigger draft...
3. **A "do nothing" exit is optional.** Leave it out when the dilemma should bite (like Scavenger's Toll).
4. **Make it personal to the faction.** The player IS that faction:
   - Terran: orders, rank, ground, discipline, the cost of holding.
   - Ascendancy: experiments, ethics, data, machines that disobey.
   - Manyworlds: community, the tide, spores, the living planet asks something back.
   - Long Tomorrow: borrowed time, burning out, saving the crew versus the mission.
5. Keep scenes to 2-3 sentences with one concrete detail.
