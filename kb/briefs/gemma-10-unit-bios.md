# Brief: unit bios for the Units screen

The new Units screen shows each of the 24 units (names in `kb/roster.md`) with its stats. Write a **bio** for each unit: 1-2 sentences, at most 160 characters, shown under its stats.

- **Voice:** a field dossier entry, written by the unit's own faction about its own soldier. Terran: clipped military. Ascendancy: clinical report. Manyworlds: oral history, warm. Long Tomorrow: crew log, weary.
- **Content:** who they are and how they fight, matching their role (leader, heavy or light) and their weapon or passive as named in `roster.md`. Don't invent numbers or abilities.
- **Format:** one line per unit: `faction/unit_key: bio text`, using the keys exactly as written in roster.md (e.g. `terran/pathfinder`). All 24, grouped by faction.

Use the proposal header from `kb/README.md`.
