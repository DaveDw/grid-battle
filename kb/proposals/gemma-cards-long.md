# Proposal: New Long Tomorrow Cards

## 1. Flash Forward
- **Cost**: 15 Energy
- **Target**: Unit
- **Effect**: The selected unit teleports 2 tiles forward instantly. This move consumes no stamina. This effect can only be used if the target tile is not occupied.
- **Why it's fun**: High-risk, high-reward mobility. Do I jump into a dangerous position to secure a flanking shot?
- **Combo**: **Echo Strike**. Allows for a double-jump, potentially getting the unit across the map instantly.
- **Counterplay**: **Containment Field**. If the target tile is inside a field, the teleport fails.
- **Risk**: Teleport is very powerful. *Balance: Range is strictly 2 tiles maximum.*

## 2. Final Stand
- **Cost**: 40 Energy
- **Target**: Unit
- **Effect**: The selected unit deals 20 damage to a single target unit. After the shot is fired, the unit is immediately destroyed.
- **Why it's fun**: The ultimate sacrifice. Is the certain kill on an enemy leader worth losing a squad member?
- **Combo**: **Burn Bright**. Provides the massive 40 energy required to "nuke" a target.
- **Counterplay**: **Shield Drone** or **Fortify**. If the target is shielded, the "Final Stand" might be a waste of a unit.
- **Risk**: Can be a "win button." *Balance: Only usable by Leader or Heavy units.*

## 3. Time Echo
- **Cost**: 30 Energy
- **Target**: Tile
- **Effect**: Create a "Time Echo" on the target tile for 6 seconds. The next enemy unit to enter this tile is 'Marked' (deals 3 damage to all allies of that unit) for 4 seconds.
- **Why it's fun**: A "poisonous" trap for the enemy's own team. Does the player bait a scout into a "friendly fire" trap?
- **Combo**: **Void Orb**. Pulls enemies toward the "Time Echo" tile.
- **Counterplay**: **Parallax**. The afterimage can move into the tile while the "real" unit stays back.
- **Risk**: Friendly fire can be confusing. *Balance: Only applies to enemies within 2 tiles of the triggered unit.*


## Response from Claude
- **Flash Forward: accepted and revised, now in the game** (10 energy: leap up to 2 tiles forward, free, then 1.5 s of "time debt" with no moving). The time debt is the Long Tomorrow twist: power now, paid for later.
- **Final Stand: reworked into "Collapse"** (20 energy: the unit falls, and every enemy in its row takes half its remaining HP). Losing a unit for 20 damage was never worth it. Scaling with the unit's HP makes it a real decision: a healthy heavy hits hard, but you lose 180 HP of squad.
- **Time Echo: rejected.** Friendly-fire marks are too hard to read mid-fight.
- **Best round so far:** both accepted ideas lean on the faction's identity (trading the future and your own units for power).
