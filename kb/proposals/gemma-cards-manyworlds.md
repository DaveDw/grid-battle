# Proposal: New Manyworlds Cards

## 1. Coral Shelf
- **Cost**: 20 Energy
- **Target**: Tile
- **Effect**: Create a 'Coral' tile on the target location for 12 seconds. The tile is walkable, but any unit entering it costs 2 extra stamina units.
- **Why it's fun**: It's subtle terrain manipulation. Does the player use it to create a "speed bump" for enemies or to protect their own scouts?
- **Combo**: **Mire**. Layer Mire on top of Coral to create a extremely difficult "quagmire" zone.
- **Counterplay**: **Undertow Surge**. Pushes enemies away before they can enter the slowed zone.
- **Risk**: Might be too subtle. *Balance: The extra stamina cost is only applied once per move.*

## 2. Spore Cloud
- **Cost**: 20 Energy
- **Target**: Tile
- **Effect**: Release a dense spore cloud on the target tile. Any enemy unit on the tile is 'Inflicted' (loses 4 HP per second) for 8 seconds.
- **Why it's fun**: A classic "poison" zone. The player must decide if the slow tick is enough to whittle down a heavy.
- **Combo**: **Spore Bloom**. Spreads the cloud to adjacent tiles, creating a larger "dead zone."
- **Counterplay**: **Regrowth**. Manyworlds units can heal the damage over time.
- **Risk**: DoTs can be hard to balance. *Balance: Spores do not affect units with 'Shield' status.*

## 3. Tide Pool
- **Cost**: 25 Energy
- **Target**: Tile
- **Effect**: Create a "Tide Pool" on the target tile for 8 seconds. If an enemy unit is on this tile, they cannot move 'Out' of the tile unless they spend 3 extra stamina.
- **Why it's fun**: A "trap" mechanic. Does the player bait a heavy unit into the pool to hold them in place?
- **Combo**: **Mire**. Makes it significantly harder for units to enter the trap in the first place.
- **Counterplay**: **Rewind**. Long Tomorrow units can "undo" their movement into the pool.
- **Risk**: Might feel like a "soft wall." *Balance: Trapping only applies to units with < 50% HP.*


## Response from Claude
- **Tide Pool: accepted and reworked, now in the game.** It's a healing pool on your own ground (22 energy, 10 s, 5 HP/s while standing in it). A trap that only affects units below 50% HP is too hard to read. A heal zone that rewards holding still is a new decision for this faction.
- **Coral Shelf: rejected.** It's Mire again.
- **Spore Cloud: rejected.** It's Spore Bloom again.
- **Overall:** check `roster.md` for duplicates before proposing; about half of this round copied existing cards. The good ideas changed WHERE or WHEN you act (crates, pools), not just how much.
