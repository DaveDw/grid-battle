# Proposal: New Terran Cards

## 1. Sentry Turret
- **Cost**: 25 Energy
- **Target**: Tile
- **Effect**: Deploy a stationary turret on the selected tile. The turret deals 6 damage to all enemies on adjacent tiles every 3 seconds for 10 seconds. After 10 seconds, the turret is destroyed.
- **Why it's fun**: It provides a persistent area-denial zone. The player must decide whether to place it in a choke point to protect a squad or near a high-value target to create a "no-go" zone.
- **Combo**: **Stake Claim**. Use Stake Claim to secure the tile the turret is on, ensuring the ground stays yours while the turret defends it.
- **Counterplay**: High-mobility Manyworlds units can use **Riptide** to move quickly into the turret's range or bypass it.
- **Risk**: If multiple turrets can be placed, it might be too strong. *Constraint: Max 2 turrets per squad.*

## 2. Deep Bunker
- **Cost**: 30 Energy
- **Target**: Unit
- **Effect**: The selected unit enters a 'Hardened' state on its current tile for 12 seconds. It cannot move, and it takes 0 damage from the front and sides. It can still fire its primary weapon.
- **Why it's fun**: It creates a "fortress" moment. The player has to decide if the unit is in a position where 'front' is protected enough to stay safe.
- **Combo**: **Fortify**. While Deep Bunker protects the sides, Fortify can be used to ensure the front is reinforced with extra damage reduction.
- **Counterplay**: **Artillery Barrage**. Since it's an area-of-effect effect, the bunker's directional protection is bypassed.
- **Risk**: Might be too good at defense. *Balance: Only works if at least 2 tiles of the squad's territory are adjacent.*

## 3. Supply Drop
- **Cost**: 15 Energy
- **Target**: Tile
- **Effect**: A supply crate lands on the target tile. The first ally unit to enter the tile gains 30 HP and 10 Energy instantly.
- **Why it's fun**: It introduces a "logistics" layer. Does the player drop it near a retreating unit or a forward-moving one?
- **Combo**: **Requisition**. Draw a card to ensure you have the Supply Drop ready for the next maneuver.
- **Counterplay**: **Containment Field**. An enemy can trap the tile to prevent your unit from reaching the drop.
- **Risk**: Healing is always a concern. 30 HP is significant for light units.


## Response from Claude
- **Supply Drop: accepted and revised, now in the game.** It costs 20 (not 15), lands on your own ground only, gives 20 HP and 15 energy, and expires after 10 s. At 15 energy for 30 HP it would beat Medpod. Your positional idea (the crate costs a move) is what made it worth building.
- **Deep Bunker: revised into "Dig In"** (20 energy, 8 s: 40% less damage taken, 25% more dealt, moving ends it). Zero damage from front and sides is total immunity in this game, because almost every attack comes from the front.
- **Sentry Turret: rejected.** It's Deploy Drone (Ascendancy) again. "Adjacent tiles" also rarely matters on a row-based battlefield.
