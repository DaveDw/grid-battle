# Proposal: Events Between Battles

This proposal outlines 10 short events that occur between battles in a sector. These events focus on trade-offs between immediate resources (Energy, Cards, HP) and future outcomes (Next Battle modifiers, Run state).

## 1. Supply Depot (Terran)
- **Scene**: A dusty, reinforced vault of rations and fuel. "Hold the Line."
- **Choices**:
  1. **Scavenge Supplies**: Gain 15 Energy, Lose 5% Squad HP.
  2. **Shore up Perimeter**: Next battle: your squad takes 10% less damage.
  3. **Keep Moving**: No effect.

## 2. Data Cache (Ascendancy)
- **Scene**: A humming, isolated server rack. "Run the Experiment."
- **Choices**:
  1. **Hack Node**: Gain a random Ascendancy card, Lose 5% Squad HP.
  2. **Remote Reboot**: Next battle: you start with 20 extra base Energy, but the enemy deck gains 1 extra card.
  3. **Leave it**: No effect.

## 3. Spore Field (Manyworlds)
- **Scene**: Vibrant, choking organic growth. "Call the Tide."
- **Choices**:
  1. **Purify Growth**: Lose 10 Energy, Heal 10% Squad HP.
  2. **Extract Spores**: Gain a random Manyworlds card, Lose 10% Squad HP.
  3. **Braving the Path**: No effect.

## 4. Time Rift (Long Tomorrow)
- **Scene**: A shuddering tear in reality. "Never Turn Back."
- **Choices**:
  1. **Forge the Future**: Gain a random Long Tomorrow card, Lose 10% Squad HP.
  2. **Anchor Reality**: Next battle: you start with 30 extra base Energy, but you lose 15% Squad HP.
  3. **Let it Pass**: No effect.

## 5. Relay Station (Neutral)
- **Scene**: A derelict, multi-frequency comms array.
- **Choices**:
  1. **Jam Enemy Comms**: Next battle: enemy starts with -10 from their base Energy.
  2. **Signal Boost**: You start with +10 to your base Energy in next battle, but the enemy deck gains 1 extra card.
  3. **Repair Array**: Heal 5% Squad HP.

## 6. Frontline Outpost (Terran)
- **Scene**: A fortified bunker with "Claim" markings. "Take the Ground."
- **Choices**:
  1. **Claim Site**: Gain 20 Energy, Lose 5% Squad HP.
  2. **Fortify**: Next battle: your squad takes 10% less damage.
  3. **Pass**: No effect.

## 7. Research Lab (Ascendancy)
- **Scene**: A flickering, high-tech lab. "Execute Protocol."
- **Choices**:
  1. **Secure Data**: Gain a random Ascendancy card, Lose 5% Squad HP.
  2. **Scorch Lab**: Next battle: enemy starts with 20% less HP.
  3. **Contain**: Heal 10% Squad HP.

## 8. Tidal Pool (Manyworlds)
- **Scene**: A shimmering, bioluminescent pool of water. "Call the Tide."
- **Choices**:
  1. **Drink**: Heal 15% Squad HP, Lose 10 Energy.
  2. **Expand Pool**: Gain a random Manyworlds card, Lose 5% Squad HP.
  3. **Leave**: No effect.

## 9. Junkyard (Long Tomorrow)
- **Scene**: A pile of twisted hull plating and wires. "Keep Them Alive."
- **Choices**:
  1. **Salvage Metal**: Gain 20 Energy, Lose 5% Squad HP.
  2. **Upgrade Scrap**: Reduce the cost of a card in your hand by 3, Lose 5% Squad HP.
  3. **Ignore**: No effect.

## 10. The Crossroads (Neutral)
- **Scene**: A clear fork in the wasteland.
- **Choices**:
  1. **Shortcut**: Skip the next battle, but the following battle is "Hard" (2x enemies).
  2. **Tactical Recon**: Next battle: you start with 3x the standard initial Energy, but the enemy deck gains 2 extra cards.
  3. **Standard Path**: No effect.

## Response from Claude
The premises were good; the choices weren't. Built 8 events from your scenes: Data Cache, Spore Field, Time Rift, Relay Station, Research Lab, Crossroads, Tidal Pool and Junkyard. All the choices were rewritten. Why:
- **Energy between battles means nothing.** The bar refills within seconds of a battle starting. "Gain 20 energy" and "start with +30 energy" do nothing. Use the effects listed in the brief.
- **Too many events were the same event:** "gain a card, lose 5% HP / small bonus / nothing" appeared seven times. Each event needs its own dilemma. For example, Tidal Pool now heals 30% but warns the enemy (+2 enemy cards); Crossroads offers an ambush (enemy -25% HP but +2 cards) or digging in (a barrier).
- **"No effect" choices** are fine as an exit, but the other choices must both tempt you and cost you.
- **Scenes:** give each one a detail that makes it a place ("its self-destruct is already counting down"), not just a label.
