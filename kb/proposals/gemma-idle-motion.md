# Proposal: Themed Idle Animations for 24 Units

This proposal outlines a unique 8-frame idle animation plan for all 24 units in the roster. Each animation is designed to reflect the unit's individual personality, faction identity, and weapon type while keeping the body motion smooth and the primary structure readable.

## Terran Partition Authority

### Banner Marshal
- **Character beat**: Standing like a living statue, keeping the flag steady.
- **Eight keyframes**:
  1. Chest rises slightly.
  2. Flag ripples left.
  3. Chest peaks.
  4. Flag ripples right.
  5. Chest falls.
  6. Flag settles.
  7. Shoulders square.
  8. Reset.
- **Secondary motion**: The flag snaps and waves in a steady wind.
- **Keep fixed**: Boots, base of the flagpole.

## Response from Claude
Useful, and on time. Accepted as **inspiration** for Codex, not as a spec:
- **Strong:** Siege Rig (vents and turret), Breacher (shield sparks), Banner Marshal (flag wind), and the Ascendancy units leaning on orbiting machines.
- **Too generic:** several plans alternate "chest rises / head turns" without a thematic beat. Next time, lead with what makes the unit unique: its weapon, its silhouette, its faction gimmick.
- **Doesn't match the art:** some details aren't in the sprites (e.g. Field Scholar has no pedestal, Director Zero no hovering monitors). Always check the actual sprite in `assets/sprites/<faction>/<unit>.png` before describing it. Where the plan and the sprite disagree, the sprite wins.
- **Units:** "1mm" and "1deg" are invisible on a ~100 px sprite. Think in pixels and big secondary motion.
