# Proposal: Commander Voice Lines

This proposal provides 3 taunts and 2 defeat lines for each of the 8 sector commanders. The lines reflect the specific leader's motto, their faction's identity, and the unique personality described in the roster.

## Banner Marshal
- **Taunts**:
  - Stand firm. This ground does not yield.
  - You are an obstacle. Orders: Hold.
  - A statue does not flinch. Proceed with caution.
- **Defeat**:
  - The line... has broken.
  - Stand... still...

## Claim Lord
- **Taunts**:
  - This sector belongs to my shadow.
  - Claiming the ground. Move or be moved.
  - You are trespassing on sovereign soil.
- **Defeat**:
  - The ground... is lost.
  - My shadow... retreats.

## Chief Visionary
- **Taunts**:
  - Your tactics are... inefficient. Let us observe.
  - The data suggests your defeat is inevitable.
  - Shall we begin the final observation phase?
- **Defeat**:
  - Data... corrupted...
  - Experiment... failed.

## Director Zero
- **Taunts**:
  - Protocol initiated. Submit to the order.
  - Correct. Your opposition is illogical.
  - Deviation detected. Termination is the only protocol.
- **Defeat**:
  - Protocol... offline.
  - System... failure.

## Tide Speaker
- **Taunts**:
  - The tide calls for your end. Let it come.
  - Flow with the current, or be swept away.
  - We are but waves in a larger sea.
- **Defeat**:
  - The tide... ebbs.
  - Pulled... under.

## Prism Elder
- **Taunts**:
  - Your light is fractured. Let me bend it.
  - Reality is a prism. Find your angle.
  - Refracted paths lead only to your end.
- **Defeat**:
  - Light... fades...
  - Dimmed...

## Mission Mother
- **Taunts**:
  - I am keeping my crew alive. You are a threat.
  - My duty is to the survivors. You are not one.
  - One more patch. One more step.
- **Defeat**:
  - Patch... complete...
  - The mission... ends.

## Captain Everafter
- **Taunts**:
  - Time is a luxury you no longer possess.
  - We have seen the end. You are merely part of it.
  - Bend to the distortion or be broken by it.
- **Defeat**:
  - Time... is out...
  - Echo... fails.

## Response from Claude
Accepted: your best round. The lines are short, in voice, and true to each leader. Three edits:
- **Banner Marshal:** "Proceed with caution" undercut him, so it's now "A statue does not flinch. Neither do I."
- **Director Zero:** "Correct." didn't land, so the line is now "Your opposition is illogical." Also, "Termination is the only protocol" became "Correction will follow" (colder, more League).
- **Mission Mother:** "Patch... complete..." read as a win, so it's now "Get them... home..."

Commander battles announce a taunt at the start and show a defeat line on the victory screen.
