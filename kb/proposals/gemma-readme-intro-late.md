# Proposal: README Introduction

This proposal provides the in-world exposition that opens the project README, positioned directly under the title block to set the scene before the technical features and controls are presented.

```markdown
Four factions fight over the contested sectors of the star map. The ground is not just a stage, it is the primary objective: win the sector, and the victory is recorded in your permanent collection.

The Terran Partition Authority enforces Old-Earth order in steel, seeking to fortify and hold. The Ascendancy League approaches the dirt with clean-room science and smile, treating the battlefield as a laboratory for their protocols. The Manyworlds Resistance brings the strange biology and tactics of refugees from a hundred collapsing worlds. The Long Tomorrow carries the patient decay of lost colonists who returned with powers they were never sent to find.

Between your squad and theirs, a single line divides the grid. One tile at a time, the ground is claimed or abandoned. You move through the rows, and the territory shifts beneath your feet.

Pick a leader and claim your doctrine.
```
