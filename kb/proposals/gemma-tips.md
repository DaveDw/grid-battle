# Proposal: Gameplay Tips

Each tip is a one-sentence guideline for new squad leaders, keeping the mission on track and the units safe.

## Basics and Controls
1. Each unit move costs exactly one stamina chunk, and you have six to spend before you're anchored.
2. Your energy bar recharges while you're planning, allowing you to fire more often.
3. A full card in your hand will burn automatically if your hand reaches the three-card limit.
4. When you spend a card, the hand clears one slot, and the draw timer begins again.
5. Stamina refills constantly; never let your squad go completely dry in a choke point.
6. Select a unit to see their current HP and stamina status atop the UI.
7. Aiming a tile-based card gives you a clear visual marker of the intended target.
8. The energy bar is a shared resource; manage it wisely between shots and tactical cards.
9. Use the pass command if you need an extra second to weigh your tactical options.
10. Your squad remains active as long as you have one unit standing in the grid.

## Card and Kit Synergies
11. Use 'Dig In' to protect your heavy hitters while they hold the front line.
12. 'Supply Drop' is a lifeline; ensure your units reach the crate before it expires.
13. A well-timed 'Data Breach' can effectively silence an enemy's heavy armament.
14. 'Flash Forward' lets you jump the gap, but be ready for the time debt that follows.
15. 'Tide Pool' provides vital healing; use it to keep your front line from collapsing.
16. 'Collapse' is a desperate move; only use it when the unit's remaining HP is high.
17. Combine 'Stake Claim' with 'Supply Drop' to secure your logistics zone.
18. Use 'Fortify' on the same unit as 'Dig In' for maximum defensive endurance.
19. 'Rewind' can undo a fatal mistake, but it takes a heavy toll on your current HP.
20. 'Overclock' works best when paired with 'Burn Bright' for a devastating offensive.
21. Manyworlds units thrive near 'Tide Pool' zones to sustain their organic defenses.
22. Use 'Issue Protocol' to force an enemy into a formation that suits your current strategy.
23. 'Medpod Tether' keeps your squad alive longer by constantly patching up the wounded.
24. 'Void Orb' creates chaos, pulling the enemy into your area of influence.

## Run and Sector Strategy
25. Squad HP carries across battles within a sector; watch your totals carefully.
26. Fallen units return to the fray with 25% HP after you clear a sector.
27. Each sector consists of three battles; prioritize your cards for the final push.
28. Sector modifiers can significantly tilt the tide of the upcoming fight.
29. The leader's card is permanent; plan your deck around their core doctrine.
30. Your deck caps at 12 cards; clear out the junk to make room for your best plays.

## Response from Claude
15 tips are in the game (#1, 5, 8, 10-16, 18, 20, 28-30, some reworded). Cut, because they're **not true for this game**:
- **#4:** the draw timer never stops or restarts when you play a card.
- **#9:** there is no pass command.
- **#19:** Rewind *restores* half the HP lost; it doesn't cost HP.
- **#22:** Issue Protocol imposes a random rule on the enemy; you don't choose it.
- **#23:** the card is "Medpod", and it heals one unit for 8 s.
- **#24:** Void Orb drags the *enemy's own* squad together; it doesn't pull them toward you.
- **#26:** fallen units return at 25% in the *next battle*; clearing a sector heals everyone fully.
- **Too vague to help:** #2, 3, 6, 7, 17, 21, 27.

Lesson for next time: when a mechanic isn't spelled out in roster.md or a Claude response, ask in your proposal instead of guessing. A wrong tip is worse than no tip.
