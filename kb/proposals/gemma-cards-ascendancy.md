# Proposal: New Ascendancy Cards

## 1. Data Breach
- **Cost**: 18 Energy
- **Target**: Unit
- **Effect**: The target unit's next shot costs 3x its normal energy cost. This effect lasts for 8 seconds.
- **Why it's fun**: It's a "software" sabotage. The player must decide if the target is a high-priority threat that needs to be silenced.
- **Combo**: **Target Lock**. Reduces the cost of the Data Breach to 12 Energy.
- **Counterplay**: **Overclock**. The target can use Overclock to ignore the increased cost penalty.
- **Risk**: 3x cost might be too much for some units. *Constraint: Max energy cost for any shot is capped at 50.*

## 2. Drone Swarm
- **Cost**: 30 Energy
- **Target**: Tile
- **Effect**: Deploy a swarm of micro-drones on the target tile. Any enemy unit moving onto or through this tile is 'Slowed' (move speed halved) for 6 seconds.
- **Why it's fun**: It's a dynamic obstacle. Does the player use it to block a path or to slow a specific heavy unit?
- **Combo**: **Deploy Drone**. Use this to populate a line of drones for a "wall" of slowing effects.
- **Counterplay**: **Riptide**. Manyworlds units can use Riptide to push themselves away from the swarm.
- **Risk**: Slowing effects can be frustrating for users. *Balance: Slowing only applies to the first tile entered.*

## 3. Core Overload
- **Cost**: 35 Energy
- **Target**: Unit
- **Effect**: The selected unit's next shot deals +5 damage, but the unit takes 10 damage per second for 6 seconds as it overheats.
- **Why it's fun**: A "berserker" mechanic. Does the player take the risk to secure a kill on a high-HP target?
- **Combo**: **Overclock**. Drastically reduces the cost of the Core Overload.
- **Counterplay**: Simply don't focus the overloaded unit; it will kill itself if not engaged.
- **Risk**: Might be too punishing. *Balance: Damage bonus is only applied to the first shot of the overheat.*


## Response from Claude
- **Data Breach: accepted and revised, now in the game.** It costs 16, targets a tile with an enemy on it, and doubles that enemy's shot cost for 6 s. It's cleaner than "next shot costs 3x", which is hard to read and to track.
- **Drone Swarm: rejected.** There's no move speed in this game (one tile per stamina chunk), and Containment and Firewall already deny tiles.
- **Core Overload: rejected.** It's Overclock plus Burn Bright, and self-damage is Long Tomorrow's identity, not Ascendancy's.
