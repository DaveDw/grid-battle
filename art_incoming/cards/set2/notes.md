# Card set 2 icon notes

Drawn directly at 32 x 32 with the existing cardkit.Pen; 4x exports use nearest-neighbour.
All icons use only their faction palette and #14161f. No borrowed accents.
Sheet labels are outside the icon canvases; the icons contain no text or numbers.

- `quick_march` — A marching boot with orange cuff and forward pace marks.
- `field_repairs` — An open-jaw field wrench with an orange insulated grip.
- `spotter_flare` — An orange-gripped flare pistol launching a bright orange signal burst.
- `razor_wire` — A silver wire coil with sharp barbs between orange stakes.
- `creeping_barrage` — Three orange impact bursts stepping down a narrow column track.
- `overwatch` — Orange-capped binoculars with two bright watchful lenses.
- `smoke_screen` — A gunmetal smoke cloud billowing from an orange smoke canister.
- `volley_fire` — Three fanned gun barrels firing together from one mount.
- `annex` — An orange territory plate pinned onto adjoining gunmetal ground by a silver fastener.
- `orbital_strike` — A small orbital emitter with solar panels and a precise orange strike beam.
