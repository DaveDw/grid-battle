# Card set 2 icons: review notes (Claude)

Icons that pass the technical checks but are clearly weak, kept for a later improvement
pass. Fix these after all four sheets are in; nothing here blocks adding the cards.

## Terran (sheet_terran.png, 2026-10-30)
- **Annex**: the most abstract icon: a grey block pressed against an orange block. It reads
  as "taking a tile" only once you know the card. Try a planted flag over a tile edge, or an
  orange tile with a chain or bolt pinning it, so it doesn't echo Stake Claim's flag.
- **Spotter Flare**: reads like a wand or sparkler. Try a flare gun firing a bright flare, or
  a tracer arc ending in a burst over a target reticle.

## Ascendancy (sheet_ascendancy.png)
- **Calibrate** and **Efficiency Pass**: both are a grey frame with a cyan lightning bolt, so
  they read as the same card and as "energy". Calibrate should say aim or precision (a
  crosshair or a dial clicking up); keep the bolt for Efficiency Pass only.

## Manyworlds (sheet_manyworlds.png)
- **Wave Rider**: the green wave is a shapeless blob under the purple board; at 32 px it
  doesn't read as riding a wave. Try a clear curling wave crest with a small surfboard or a
  figure on top, plus up/down arrows for the two-row move.
- **Tsunami**: the silhouette reads like a pointed hat. Make it wider and lower, a wall of
  water breaking forward with foam along the top, so it's clearly bigger than Undertow Surge.

## Long Tomorrow (sheet_long.png)
- **Slow Burn**: a cracked red lump with a tiny gold spark. It reads as a rock or a steak,
  not fire. Try a smouldering tile with low flames, or a fuse burning down.
- **Afterglow**: a red stick with a gold blob on the end; unclear what it shows. The card
  heals per shot fired: try a muzzle flash that turns into a heart, or a warm glow around
  a small cross.

## Second look (after Astra's revision pass, 19:13)
Astra read these notes and redrew all eight. Re-checked: all 40 still pass the technical
rules, and each fix reads clearly now:
- Annex: an orange plate pinned onto gunmetal ground by a fastener. Clear enough; the most
  abstract icon left, but no longer weak.
- Spotter Flare: a flare pistol firing a bright burst.
- Calibrate: a gunsight with a cyan reticle (no bolt). Efficiency Pass keeps the bolt.
- Wave Rider: a surfboard on a compact curling wave.
- Tsunami: a broad breaking wave over purple undertow.
- Slow Burn: a tile smouldering under low flames.
- Afterglow: a handgun whose muzzle flash rises into a heart.
Nothing outstanding.
